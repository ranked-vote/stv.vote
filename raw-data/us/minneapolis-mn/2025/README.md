# Minneapolis, Minnesota — November 2025

This directory contains the official cast vote records for Minneapolis's two
multi-winner ranked-choice voting contests:

- Park & Recreation Commissioner At Large, 3 seats
- Board of Estimate and Taxation, 2 seats

The original CSV files were downloaded from the City of Minneapolis on
2026-07-28 and compressed without modification.

Sources:

- <https://vote.minneapolismn.gov/results-data/election-results/2025/park-board-at-large/>
- <https://vote.minneapolismn.gov/results-data/election-results/2025/bet/>
